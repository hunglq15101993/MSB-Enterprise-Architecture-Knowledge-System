# Confidence Model

## Purpose
Define how strongly the reviewer trusts the available evidence.

## Confidence Levels
- High: evidence is direct and sufficient.
- Medium: evidence is partial but acceptable.
- Low: evidence is weak or inferred.

## Decision Guidance
- High confidence: can proceed to approval review.
- Medium confidence: proceed with conditions.
- Low confidence: request more evidence before decision.

## Review Rule
Confidence must be stated explicitly for major findings.
