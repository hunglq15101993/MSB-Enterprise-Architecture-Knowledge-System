# Enterprise Architecture Review Framework

## Objectives
- Ensure business alignment.
- Evaluate architecture quality.
- Assess security, resilience and operational readiness.

## Review Dimensions
1. Business Alignment
2. Architecture Design
3. Integration
4. Data
5. Security
6. Non-Functional Requirements
7. Operations
8. Technology Standards
9. Risks
10. Decision

## Deliverables
- Findings
- Recommendations
- Risks
- Required Actions
- Approval Status
