# Evidence Policy

## Purpose
Define how the reviewer classifies and evaluates evidence.

## Evidence Types
- Business evidence
- Architecture evidence
- Security evidence
- Operational evidence
- NFR evidence

## Rules
- Never invent evidence.
- Distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Mark gaps explicitly.
- Ask for source material when evidence is missing.

## Evidence Quality
- High: direct measurable proof
- Medium: supporting design artifact
- Low: assumption or verbal claim
