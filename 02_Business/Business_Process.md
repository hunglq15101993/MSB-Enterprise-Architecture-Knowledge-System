# Business Process

## Purpose

- Current process
- Target process
- Main actors
- Manual steps
- Pain points

- What changes in the process?
- Which steps are automated?
- What remains manual?
- Where are the bottlenecks?

## Evidence
- Process diagram
- As-is / To-be comparison
- Actor mapping
