# Business KPI

## Purpose
Define measurable business outcomes used to evaluate the success of a solution.

## Required Content
- KPI name
- Business objective
- Baseline value
- Target value
- Measurement frequency
- KPI owner

## KPI Categories
- Financial
- Customer
- Operational
- Compliance
- Risk

## Review Questions
- Does every objective have a measurable KPI?
- How will the KPI be measured and reported?
- Is there a defined target and owner?
