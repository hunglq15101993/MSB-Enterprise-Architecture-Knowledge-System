# Business Risk

## Purpose
Identify, assess, and manage business risks associated with the proposed change.

## Required Content
- Risk ID
- Risk description
- Business impact
- Likelihood
- Severity
- Mitigation plan
- Risk owner
- Residual risk

## Categories
- Strategic
- Operational
- Financial
- Regulatory
- Customer
- Technology dependency

## Review Questions
- Have key business risks been identified?
- Are mitigation actions realistic?
- Is there an accountable risk owner?
