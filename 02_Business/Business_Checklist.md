# Business Checklist

## Checklist
- [ ] Business objective is clear
- [ ] Current pain point is described
- [ ] Scope is defined
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable
- [ ] Stakeholders are identified
- [ ] Capability mapping exists
- [ ] Key business risks are listed
- [ ] Requirement traceability is defined

## Review Rule
If any critical item is missing, request clarification before approval.
