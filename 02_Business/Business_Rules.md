# Business Rules

## Purpose
Capture the business rules that influence the solution design.

## Required Content
- Rule description
- Rule owner
- Source of truth
- Impacted process
- Impacted capability

## Review Questions
- Is the rule explicit and unambiguous?
- Who owns the rule?
- Can the rule change independently?
- How is the rule enforced?

## Evidence
- Policy document
- Process rulebook
- Owner confirmation
