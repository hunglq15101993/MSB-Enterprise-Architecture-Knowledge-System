# Business Requirement

## Purpose
Capture the business objective and the reason for change.

## Required Content
- Business objective
- Current pain point
- Scope
- Success criteria
- Stakeholders

## Review Questions
- What problem is being solved?
- Why is this change needed now?
- Who benefits from the change?
- How will success be measured?

## Evidence
- Business case
- Process gap
- KPI target
- Regulatory need
