# Business Capability

## Purpose
Describe the business capabilities affected by the change.

## Required Content
- Existing capability
- New capability
- Modified capability
- Capability owner
- Reuse opportunities

## Review Questions
- Which capability is changing?
- Is the capability already available elsewhere?
- Who owns the capability?
- What is new vs reused?

## Evidence
- Capability map
- Ownership mapping
- Reuse analysis
