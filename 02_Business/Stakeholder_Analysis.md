# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose
Identify stakeholders affected by the business change and define their responsibilities.

## Stakeholder Categories
- Business Sponsor
- Product Owner
- Business User
- Enterprise Architect
- Operations
- Security
- Compliance

## Required Content
- Stakeholder
- Role
- Interest
- Influence
- Responsibilities
- Communication approach

## Review Questions
- Are all key stakeholders identified?
- Who approves the business outcome?
- Who owns post-implementation operations?
